# R187 surrender and prisoner-state consistency

Surrender previously copied every affiliated officer into a city roster regardless of captive status. An officer already held by the new force became its own captive and appeared in both serving and captive lists. A surrendered force's prisoners also retained a dead captor until a later roster reconciliation.

When affiliation transfers, an officer held by the new force becomes serving and loses captive fields. Officers held by a third force remain its prisoners, change affiliation only and are not added to serving rosters. After the surrendered force is marked dead, the existing roster/dead-captor reconciliation runs immediately, using the same release rule as load. Existing field officers remain in inherited units, not city rosters. This does not release all third-party prisoners or change surrender probability, price, target selection, diplomacy policy or economy.
